# Ecological Interaction

Ecological Interaction develops open research, computational
methods, and digital infrastructure for understanding and
working with ecological systems.

Our work connects ecological observation, scientific knowledge,
computation, intelligence, infrastructure, and design.

## Research Areas

### Ecological Understanding
Education, courseware, and ecological research methods.

### Ecological Information
Scientific references, knowledge bases, and environmental datasets.

### Ecological Computation
Open-source tools for processing and analysing ecological information.

### Ecological Intelligence
Indicators, interpretation, modelling, and decision-support systems.

### Ecological Infrastructure
Digital systems supporting distributed ecological research and practice.

### Ecological Generation
Design, experimentation, and the development of ecological interventions.

## Projects

Our repositories contain research documentation, scientific
databases, computational tools, and educational resources.

Individual projects provide their own documentation and
installation instructions.
